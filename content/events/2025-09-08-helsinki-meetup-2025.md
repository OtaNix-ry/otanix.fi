OtaNix ry organized a Nix Meetup in Finland on the 8th of September 2025 together with [Nitor](https://nitor.com). 
The meetup was the successor for the [first Finnish Nix meetup](https://fi.nix.ug/meetups/first-meetup/) organized a year ago. Food, snacks, and drinks were served to the attendants. 

## Presentations
Apart from networking, the meetup featured presentations on various interesting Nix related topics. Some of the topics are listed below:
- A showcase of ephemeral rootfs setups with flakes by [Juuso](https://github.com/jhvst)
- Migrating from Arch to NixOS by [Jaakko](https://github.com/Jaakkonen)
- The `meow` cli tool by [Sergey](https://github.com/PixelSergey)
- Running desktop apps in VMs, qubes/spectrumos-style by [SomeoneSerge](https://github.com/SomeoneSerge)
- Rust & Nix by [Luukas](https://github.com/lajp)

Stay tuned for upcoming Nix related events organized by OtaNix ry!
