## Meetup announcement

OtaNix ry and Nitor welcome you to the 2nd ever Nix meetup in Finland. The meetup is held at the Nitor office at Aleksanterinkatu 46 A, Helsinki on the 8th of September starting at 17:00.

Food and refreshments will be served during the event. We open the floor for anyone in the community to give a short presentation. If you'd like to give a presentation, please mention it in the sign up form.
